# AlloCiné plugin images

`allocine-plugin-logo.png` is an unchanged PNG rendering of
[Allocine square icon.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Allocine_square_icon.svg)
by Allociné and Wikimedia Commons user Editantpv.

The image is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

`allocine-classiques.svg`, `allocine-club-aime.svg`, `allocine-les-indes.svg`, and `allocine-club-scream.svg` are official AlloCiné editorial pills from `assets.allocine.fr`. Classiques already includes its gold rounded container. A dark rounded container is added around the Club Aime and Les Indés marks so they match the live AlloCiné gelules on light and dark themes. Club Scream is the official PNG pill clipped to a rounded SVG container.

service supported by this unofficial plugin and does not imply endorsement.
